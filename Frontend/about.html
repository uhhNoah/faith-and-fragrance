Meet the creators

Behind Faith & Fragrance Co.

A young husband-and-wife team, building a brand rooted in calm, quiet luxury, and a shared devotion to Christ.

Faith & Fragrance Co. started in a season where life felt loud, busy, and a little heavier than it should have. Between work, family, and everything else, our home didn’t always feel as peaceful as we wanted it to be. We began looking for small, intentional ways to slow down, reconnect, and invite peace back into our space.

One of those ways was fragrance — simple evenings testing wax melts and candles, talking, and turning the noise down. Over time, that practice became more than a hobby. It became a reminder that God still meets us in the quiet, everyday moments, and that home should feel like a place where you can breathe again.

Founders
Young, married & building with faith

We’re young, still growing, still learning, and still becoming who God wants us to be. Through it all, we’re devoted to Christ and committed to building something that reflects His peace, His grace, and His presence in everyday life.

Every product we make is shaped by that — not to preach at anyone, but to quietly carry faith, comfort, and intention into the spaces where real life happens.

If our products bring even a moment of stillness or comfort into your day, then this brand has already done what we hoped it would.

Story

How this all started

We didn’t build Faith & Fragrance Co. to look perfect on a shelf — we built it for evenings where the house is finally quiet, the lights are low, and you can feel the stress of the day start to lift just a little.

We know what it’s like to juggle long shifts, big responsibilities, little ones who need you, and a calling that requires your whole heart. That’s why our brand is designed to feel simple, peaceful, and honest — never overwhelming, never overcomplicated.

This is our way of saying: you are allowed to have a home that feels soft, even when life doesn’t. A space that smells like rest, even when everything else feels like work.

Values

What we care about most

Value
Faith first

Our faith in Christ shapes how we treat people, how we build this brand, and why we care so much about creating products that bring comfort and peace instead of more noise.
